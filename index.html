What we do

We work with maintenance contractors and owners of transportation infrastructure, connecting GIS data, field crews and reporting in one place.

1

Asset management

Asset inventories on ArcGIS, with inspections, failures, repairs and quality control linked to each asset.

2

Field operations

Work planning, crew assignments, daily work logs and mobile tools that work on site, even without signal.

3

Inspection & reporting

Standards-based inspection forms, bridge inspection programs, maintenance of traffic plans and client-ready reports.

Our work

ASOLENZARA developed and publishes the WWB Asset Management application, used by the maintenance team of the Woodrow Wilson Bridge corridor (I-95 / I-495, Virginia and Maryland).

  • Inventory of bridge corridor assets on ArcGIS Online
  • Inspections, failures, repairs and QC checklists per asset
  • Work plans, crew assignments and JAMMS work order links
  • Bridge inspection program and MOT layouts
  • Daily work assignments sent to field crews by email and text
