Program

WWB Daily Work Assignments by ASOLENZARA LLC sends employees and supervisors on the Woodrow Wilson Bridge maintenance project their daily work assignments by text message from +1 (844) 320-7676.

Consent

Messages are sent only to employees who gave their mobile number and agreed, by signed consent form or verbally to their supervisor. Consent is not a condition of employment.

Frequency

Usually one message per workday; occasionally more when the day's plan changes.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Stop and help

Reply STOP to stop receiving messages; you will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to receive them again. Reply HELP for help, or contact info@asolenzara.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our privacy policy. Mobile numbers and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing.
