How employees opt in to work-assignment texts

ASOLENZARA LLC sends its own field employees and supervisors on the Woodrow Wilson Bridge maintenance project their daily work assignment by text message. These are operational messages only. No marketing or promotional content is ever sent.

How employees opt inConsent formSMS termsPrivacy policy

Who receives the messages

Only employees and supervisors assigned to the project who have given their own mobile phone number for this purpose and agreed to receive work-assignment texts.

How consent is given

  1. When an employee joins the project, their supervisor gives them the SMS consent form.
  2. The employee writes their mobile number, ticks the consent box and signs. The form states what will be sent, how often, that message and data rates may apply, and how to stop (reply STOP) or get help (reply HELP).
  3. The supervisor enters the number in the company's work-planning application. Only numbers with a signed form are entered.
  4. Employees may also agree verbally to their supervisor. The supervisor then records the consent with the date in the work-planning application, after reading out the same terms.

Verbal consent script

When consent is given verbally, the supervisor reads this to the employee and records the answer and date in the work-planning application:

Supervisor: "As part of your work on the Woodrow Wilson Bridge project, ASOLENZARA LLC can send you a text message each workday from (844) 320-7676 with your work assignment for the day: where, what assets, your team and supervisor. Usually one message per workday, sometimes more if the plan changes. Message and data rates may apply. You can get help any time by replying HELP, and you can stop the texts completely by replying STOP. This is not a condition of your employment. The SMS terms are at asolenzara.com/sms/terms.html and the privacy policy at asolenzara.com/sms/privacy.html. Do you agree to receive these work-assignment texts at this mobile number? Please answer yes or no."
Employee: "Yes."
Supervisor: "Thank you. You will receive your first work-assignment text on your next workday."

If the employee says no, their number is not entered and they receive their assignments from their supervisor in person.

What is sent

One message per workday, at the start of the day, sometimes a second one when the day's plan changes. It lists the employee's assigned operations: location, assets to work on, team, supervisor, equipment and traffic control.

Example:
WWB work plan · Nov 17, 2026
Jose Rivera: 2 operations today.
1) POP-26-090-01 · Guardrail repair · you: Lead
Where: Near Telegraph Rd · I-95 NB MM 1.2–1.8
With: Ana Flores · Supervisor: K. Okafor
Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg&data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop immediately, or tell your supervisor. Reply HELP for help, or email info@asolenzara.com.
